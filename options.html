Image Format Saver

Options

Right-click any picture and pick Save image as JPG, PNG or WebP, then the format you want. Or pick Copy as PNG.

Pictures from other sites
Some sites keep their pictures on a separate server that blocks other apps from reading them. If a picture will not convert, allow this. You can turn it off again here.

Private by design. Pictures are converted on your computer. Image Format Saver has no account, no analytics and sends nothing anywhere. It only reads a picture when you right-click it and choose an option.
